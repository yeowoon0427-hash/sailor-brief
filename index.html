How it works

01

Measure

Conflict, coercion, sanction and military events between a named pair of countries, counted per 100,000 world events so a busy news week doesn't inflate everything.

02

Compare

Each measure against that situation's own trailing 26 weeks. Unusual means unusual for that situation — not loud, not dramatic.

03

Translate

Which of your locations sit inside it, what comparable companies did in past cases of the same shape, and a draft of what each team should do.

What it can't do

Stated here rather than discovered later.

  • Eleven situations, not the world. Anything outside these actor pairs is invisible, and Sailor says so rather than guessing.
  • Levels are computed. The words are not. The scoring is automatic; the wording around it is written by a person. We would rather say that than generate something we cannot source.
  • Thin weeks are withheld, not scored. When world event volume collapses, every rate inflates. Those weeks are discarded instead of being reported as an escalation.
  • Relative, not absolute. Alert means unusual for that situation. It does not mean dangerous in itself.
  • Rulemaking is the weak category. 2 of 4 in the backtest, and one pre-registered forecast re-tests exactly that.
