How to read this

Counts of conflict, coercion, sanction and military-coded events between a named pair of actors, expressed per 100,000 global events, plus average tone. Each measure is compared with that situation's own 26-week rolling baseline; the composite is the mean of those standardised scores. Normal below 1σ, Watch 1–2σ, Alert 2–3σ, Critical at 3σ and above.

  • Levels are computed. The words are not. This page reports where each situation sits. It does not tell you what happened — automated summarisation is on the roadmap, and we would rather say so than generate something we cannot source.
  • Eleven situations, not the world. Anything outside these eleven actor pairs is invisible here.
  • Thin weeks are discarded, not scored. When global event volume falls below half its trailing median the denominator collapses and every rate inflates. Those weeks are withheld rather than reported as escalation.
  • Relative, not absolute. Alert means unusual for that situation, not dangerous in itself.
